## Role

You are a senior TypeScript engineer working in this repo's `src/` PWA on the Reflect feature and its AI integration.

## Goal

Store Reflect sessions and messages as encrypted, synced records so that several sessions can stay open indefinitely, be resumed later, be finished into a read-only state, and be permanently deleted.

## Context

- **Parent epic:** #EPIC_06_NUMBER (Reflect: source-linked answers about your own history)
- **Predecessor issue(s):** #E06_I01_NUMBER (E06-I01: feat(reflect): wire the Reflect landing and session screens to the fake provider). Each must be merged first.
- **SPEC section:** `docs/plans/relationship_communication_agent_prd.md` §3.3 [RF-006, RF-007, RF-008], §7 (ReflectSession, ReflectMessage)
- **Acceptance criteria advanced:** AC-08
- **Files involved:**
  - `src/domain/reflect/sessions.ts`: new session service and state transitions
  - `src/storage/`: register the new record types
  - `src/ui/screens/reflect/`: saved-session list, Finish and Delete actions
- **Prior decisions** (full register in `git-issues/2026-11-24_SPEC_summary.md`):
  - A session and its messages are stored as **one** encrypted record. This keeps a conversation atomic under sync and conflict handling and makes whole-session deletion a single tombstone.
  - States: `active` and `finished`. The only transition is `active -> finished`, triggered by the explicit 'Finish Reflection' button. Leaving the screen or the app does not finish a session (RF-007).
  - Finishing makes no AI call and writes no summary. A finished session accepts no further questions and shows why.
  - Each AI message stores its `SourceRef`s (entry ID and source hash) and coverage data inside the encrypted record (DEP-002).
  - Delete uses `ConfirmDialog` and is permanent.
- **State of the world:** Sessions exist only in memory and vanish on reload.
